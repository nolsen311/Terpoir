# Trademark Policy

"Terpoir," the Terpoir logo, and the terpoir.app domain identify the official
Terpoir app and project. The code is free software under the GPL (see
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)); this policy covers only the name and marks.

## Why this exists

Terpoir protects sensitive personal data. A modified build distributed under
the Terpoir name could quietly weaken those protections or steal users'
recovery keys. Users must be able to trust that anything called "Terpoir" is
the genuine, verifiable build.

## What you may do without asking

- Refer to Terpoir by name to describe it, discuss it, review it, or say your
  project is compatible with or based on it ("a fork of Terpoir").
- Use the name in unmodified redistributions of official releases, with their
  original signatures intact.

## What requires written permission

- Distributing a modified version under the name "Terpoir" or any confusingly
  similar name, or with the Terpoir logo or icon.
- Using the name or logo in an app store listing, domain name, or account name
  for anything other than the official project.
- Implying endorsement by or affiliation with the Terpoir project.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the GPL. Give yours a distinct name and icon, and make
it clear it is not the official Terpoir app.

## Official builds

Only builds signed with the keys published on https://terpoir.app are
official. Signing-certificate fingerprints and reproducible-build hashes will
be listed there and in [README.md](README.md) once releases begin.

## Reporting misuse

Report impersonating apps or listings through GitHub (open an issue, or use a
private security advisory if the copy appears malicious).
